Powerful Features

Everything you need to manage your tabs efficiently

↔

Horizontal Scrolling

See all your tabs in a horizontal layout. No more squinting at tiny tab titles or losing track of open pages.

  • Smooth horizontal scroll
  • Mouse wheel support
  • Adjustable scroll speed
◎

Auto-Focus

Never lose track of where you are. The active tab is automatically centered and highlighted when you open the popup.

  • Smart auto-centering
  • Visual active indicator
  • One-click tab switching
□

Tab Groups

Native Chrome tab group support. View your grouped tabs organized together with their custom colors.

  • Group visualization
  • Collapsible sections
  • Pinned tabs section
⚡

Quick Actions

Access powerful tab management features right from the popup. Pin, mute, duplicate, or close tabs instantly.

  • Pin/unpin tabs
  • Mute/unmute audio
  • Duplicate, open new tab, close
◈

Customizable

Make it yours. Adjust the popup size, toggle features, and customize the experience to match your workflow.

  • Adjustable popup width
  • Show/hide tab titles
  • Tab counter display
